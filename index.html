🕉️ Acharya aur Peeth se Judein

Dharm ka sarvashreshtha stambh aacharya hain. Sri Shankaracharya dwara sthapit 4 aamna peethon ke shreshta aacharyon ke saath-sath anek sampradaya ke aacharya logon ke jeevan mein dharmik samasyaon ka hal uplabdh karate hain. Un sabhi ka parichay aur margadarshan ke liye hamara bhaiya manch dekhein:

🛕
Govardhan Peeth (Puri)
Adi Shankaracharya dwara sthapit charam dhamon mein se ek. Sanyog aur gyan ki parampara.
shivbodhtrust.org par parichayein →
🪔

shivbodhtrust.org par parichayein →
🙏
Jyotir Peeth (Dwarika)
