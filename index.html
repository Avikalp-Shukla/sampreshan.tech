🕉️ Connect with Acharyas and Peeths

Acharyas are the highest pillar of Dharma. Along with the great acharyas of the 4 sacred peeths established by Sri Adi Shankaracharya, acharyas of many traditions solve religious problems in people's lives. For their introduction and guidance, see our sister platform:

🛕
Govardhan Peeth (Puri)
One of the four dhams established by Adi Shankaracharya. Tradition of renunciation and wisdom.
Read on shivbodhtrust.org →
🪔
Sharada Peeth (Sringeri)
The foremost seat of Advaita Vedanta. An ancient centre of Vedic wisdom and philosophy.
Read on shivbodhtrust.org →
🙏
Dwaraka Peeth (Sharda)
The religious seat of the western region. Guidance of the acharyas.
Read on shivbodhtrust.org →
📿
Jyotirmath Peeth (Badri)
The northern Himalayan seat. The traditional home of renunciation and contemplation.
Read on shivbodhtrust.org →
